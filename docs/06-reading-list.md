# 6. Reading list

A curated starting set, grouped by topic. Entries are primary sources or widely used
reviews; each is a candidate for the paper-reproduction tracker in phase 8. Venue
abbreviations: JPS = Journal of Power Sources; JES = Journal of The Electrochemical Society;
RSER = Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews.

## Reviews and perspectives

- Berecibar, Gandiaga, Villarreal, Omar, Van Mierlo, Van den Bossche (2016). Critical review
  of state of health estimation methods of Li-ion batteries for real applications. RSER.
- Li, Liu, Foley, Zülke, Berecibar, Nanini-Maury, Van Mierlo, Hoster (2019). Data-driven
  health estimation and lifetime prediction of lithium-ion batteries: A review. RSER.
- Hu, Xu, Lin, Pecht (2020). Battery lifetime prognostics. Joule.
- Lucu, Martinez-Laserna, Gandiaga, Camblong (2018). A critical review on self-adaptive
  Li-ion battery ageing models. JPS.
- Edge et al. (2021). Lithium ion battery degradation: what you cannot see. Physical
  Chemistry Chemical Physics.
- O'Kane et al. (2022). Lithium-ion battery degradation: how to model it. Physical Chemistry
  Chemical Physics.
- Attia et al. (2022). Review: "Knees" in lithium-ion battery aging trajectories. JES.
- Sulzer et al. (2021). The challenge and opportunity of battery lifetime prediction from
  field data. Joule.
- Aykol et al. (2021). Perspective: Combining physics and machine learning to predict
  battery lifetime. JES.
- Reniers, Mulder, Howey (2019). Review and performance comparison of mechanical-chemical
  degradation models for lithium-ion batteries. JES.
- Andersson et al. (2022). Parametrization of physics-based battery models from input-output

- Brosa Planella et al. (2022). A continuum of physics-based lithium-ion battery models

- Navidi, Thelen, Li, Hu (2024). Physics-informed machine learning for battery degradation

  batteries. Journal of Electrochemical Energy Conversion and Storage.

## Tools

- PyBaMM: physics-based battery simulation.
- PyBOP: parameter estimation and design optimisation on top of PyBaMM.
- BEEP (Toyota Research Institute): battery evaluation and early prediction pipeline
  (Herring et al., 2020, SoftwareX).
- cellpy: reading and processing cycler data.
- PyProBE (Imperial College London): Polars-based processing of cycler data with ICA/DVA,
  pseudo-OCV fitting and SOH summaries.
- impedance.py: equivalent-circuit fitting of impedance spectra (Murbach et al., 2020,
  JOSS).
- galvani: parsing Bio-Logic files.
- Battery Data Format (LF Energy Battery Data Alliance): an open interoperability standard
  worth aligning the canonical schema with.
- Battery Archive, BatteryML, BatteryLife: see section 4.2.
